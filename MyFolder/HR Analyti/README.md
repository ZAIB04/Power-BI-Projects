HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce trends using an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI.
The goal is to help HR teams make data-driven decisions by identifying key factors behind employee turnover.

🛠 Tools Used

Power BI

Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)

📈 Key Insights & Features

Overall employee count, attrition, and attrition rate

Attrition analysis by Gender, Age Group, Education, Job Role, Salary Slab, and Years at Company

Job Satisfaction matrix for different roles

Department-level filtering for deeper analysis

Clean, professional UI with business-focused visuals

💡 Key Findings

Higher attrition observed in lower salary slabs

Employees with fewer years at the company show higher turnover

Certain job roles and age groups are more prone to attrition

🎯 Skills Demonstrated

Data Cleaning & Transformation

HR Analytics

Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

Business Insight Generation

📌 Outcome
This dashboard enables HR professionals to identify risk areas, improve retention strategies, and support strategic workforce planning.
